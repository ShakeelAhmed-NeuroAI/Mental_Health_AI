1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & THE CLINICAL OPPORTUNITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current neuropsychiatric screening models are restricted by subjective symptom 
reporting and expensive, hard-to-scale diagnostic loops. Mental disorders—
specifically Major Depressive Disorder, Severe Anxiety, Schizophrenia, and 
Bipolar Disorder—manifest as localized and long-range circuit desynchronizations 
across structural brain networks rather than simple chemical imbalances.

This project delivers a high-throughput, offline-capable Deep Learning software 
pipeline that ingests standard multi-channel electrophysiology recordings, maps 
them onto anatomical graph frameworks, and computes precise neural network equilibrium 
indices within milliseconds. By solving the data-decoding bottleneck, this architecture 
provides an objective, math-driven screening dashboard for diagnostic acceleration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE TECHNOLOGICAL ADVANCEMENTS DEVELOPED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The proprietary workstation sandbox architecture implements three core computational pillars:
  * Ingestion Data Layer: Linked dynamic row-by-row clinical CSV arrays processing 
    50 independent multi-parameter patient profiles simultaneously.
  * Spatial Graph Layer: Structured custom Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) layers 
    computing spatial messaging pathways across 68 global brain coordinate nodes 
    linked to explicit structural adjacency tracks.
  * Optimization Convergence: Implemented cross-entropy backpropagation loops 
    training spatial node tracks cleanly down to a loss baseline of 0.5278.
